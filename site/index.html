Skogseidvatnet

A printable fishing chart for the lake in Bjørnafjorden. Print it, laminate it and take it in the boat.

Download PDF A3 · 0.7 MB

Printing

  • Print at A3 for 1:20 000.
  • On A4, choose "fit to page". The scale bar stays correct at any size.
  • Laminate it, then write in the catch log with a marker.
