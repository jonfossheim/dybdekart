Skogseidvatnet · NVE 2043 · Bjørnafjorden

Four directions for the lake map

Each mockup uses the real depth contours from Skogheim (1983), the only depth map of this lake. I took them from the vector figure in Rådgivende Biologer report 3892. The fish farm pens are at their registered positions in Fiskeridirektoratet's database. Pick a direction, or mix parts of two, and I'll build it.

A

Printed fishing chart

What it is
A sea-chart-style sheet. Shallow water is shaded darkest, as on Kartverket charts, because the shallows are where you fish. Numbered spots tie to notes in the margin. You print it, laminate it and take it in the boat.
Good for
Planning around a table. It works with no signal and no battery.
Gives up
It can't show your own position or record catches.

B

On-the-water sonar

What it is
A phone app for the boat. It shows a big depth readout under your GPS position, the track you've driven, and a warning when you get within 100 m of a pen. One tap logs a catch at your position.
Good for
Finding the 20 m edge and staying on it while you troll.
Gives up
It needs the phone and GPS. The depth readout is only as good as the 1983 map until we add our own sonar data.

C

Trip planner

What it is
Choose a month and a species. The map highlights the depth band where those fish are likely to be, based on the lake's temperature profile. Spots are ranked, and each one gives its reason and source.
Good for
Deciding before the trip where to fish, at what depth and with what.
Gives up
The highlighted band is our hypothesis from the profile and catch reports. It's not a guarantee.

Data behind the mockups
  • Depth contours: Skogheim 1983, from Rådgivende Biologer report 3892 (2023). NIVA (Hobæk 1994) recorded depths down to 129 m, so the deepest part of the old map is probably too shallow.
  • Shoreline: NVE Innsjødatabase, lake 2043. NVE has no digital depth map for this lake.
  • Pen positions: Fiskeridirektoratet API, sites 12042, 12096, 27956 and 28796. The 1983 map is aligned to NVE's shoreline to within about 70 m, which is enough for mockups. The real build will be aligned more precisely.
  • Catches: flue.no (record char), fiskeavisen.no (6 kg char at 20 m), Rådgivende Biologer 3478 (net survey: 411 trout, 21 char, 17 salmon).
  • The rules sources disagree on daily quota, minimum size and net fishing. Check with Hålandsdalen Grunneigarlag on inatur.no before the trip.
