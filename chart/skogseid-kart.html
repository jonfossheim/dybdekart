Spots

  1. 1
    Orraelva inflow Top of the long northeast arm, 0–40 m deep. Trout feed where the river comes in, especially in spring and after rain. The river itself is closed to fishing.
  2. 2
    North pens Pens II and III next to the Eidselva inflow. Spilled feed draws big char and trout. Fish the drift side, outside the 100 m zone.
  3. 3
    Ospenes pen East shore pen. Same feed effect as spot 2.
  4. 4
    Underwater hill Top about 52 m, roughly 30 m above the bottom around it. Jig or troll deep along the slopes for char.
  5. 5
    Deepest basin 115 m on the 1983 map; NIVA measured 129 m by sonar. Below 20 m the water stays near 4 °C.
  6. 6
    Island shelves Shallow shelves (0–20 m) around the islands. Trout cruise here in spring and autumn: spinners, wobblers, flies.
  7. 7
    Skogseidelva inflow The 2021 survey found a high density of trout in Skogseidelva. Try the mouth and the drop-off outside it.
  8. 8
    South pen Pen at the south tip, near the Tomraelva inflow. Same feed effect as spot 2.

Fish

  • Brown trout, Arctic char and escaped farmed salmon. A 2020–21 net survey caught 411 trout, 21 char and 17 salmon.
  • Norwegian record char: 8.285 kg (2002). A 6 kg char was taken through the ice at 20 m in March 2018, on a black and yellow spoon with maggot.
  • Reported for big char: 100 mm Dividal spoons with UV, maggot or PowerBait, sinking fly lines. Fish near the pens.

Water

  • Summer and autumn: warm top layer down to 15–20 m, then a sharp drop to about 4 °C to the bottom. Char often hold just below that drop.
  • Oxygen is good all the way down. Visibility about 6 m.
  • The lake drains west to Henangervatnet.

Rules

  • Fishing card on inatur.no (Hålandsdalen Grunneigarlag). Report your catch.
  • Keep at least 100 m from the pens. Max 6 hp. Wear life vests.
  • Release trout over 3 kg. Keep big char.
  • Sources disagree on daily quota, minimum size and net fishing. Check before you go.
